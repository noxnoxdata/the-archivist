# Character Sheets: The Archive of Quiet Things

*Part 1: The Boy Without a Book. Manga reference sheets. Keep the silhouettes and outfits consistent across every panel.*

---

## Hina Aizawa

- **Age:** 17
- **Height:** 160 cm
- **Role:** Protagonist, archive keeper in training
- **Silhouette:** Small and upright. Hands usually in her apron pockets or holding a book. Hair tied back. She takes up less space than she should.
- **Hair:** Black, straight, tied back with a plain dark ribbon. A few strands escape when she is tired.
- **Eyes:** Grey. Flat and observant by default. They only go wide when she is caught off guard.
- **Face:** Round, serious, rarely animated. A small mole under the left eye.
- **Outfit:** Faded navy work apron over a cream sweater, dark trousers, worn sandals. Ink stains on her fingers.
- **Key prop:** A roll of masking tape, a pencil behind her ear, a lantern.

**Expression sheet**
- **Neutral:** brows level, mouth a flat line, eyes doing most of the work.
- **Annoyed:** one brow up, mouth slightly down, a long exhale.
- **Softened:** eyes lower, mouth barely lifts. This is her real smile. Use it once per chapter, no more.
- **Scared:** eyes wide, hands still. She freezes before she moves.
- **Caught off guard:** a half step back, one hand reaching for something solid.

**Manga notes**
- Draw her small against tall shelves and wide sea. The negative space is the point.
- Her hands are expressive. Use close-ups of ink-stained fingers on paper.
- Never make her cute for the sake of it. She is quiet, not sweet.

## Ren

- **Age:** appears 17
- **Height:** 178 cm
- **Role:** The boy without a book
- **Silhouette:** Tall and still. Hands in his coat pockets. The coat is too big, as if it belonged to someone else.
- **Hair:** Black, slightly long, falling over one eye.
- **Eyes:** Dark brown, almost black. Calm, tired, a little too old.
- **Face:** Angular, gentle. A small scar on the right jaw that he cannot explain.
- **Outfit:** A faded blue coat, too warm for the season, over a white shirt, barefoot. The coat never looks wet, even in rain.
- **Key visual:** His shadow lags half a second behind him. Show it in wide shots, never explain it in dialogue in Part 1.

**Expression sheet**
- **Calm:** eyes soft, mouth neutral. His default.
- **Curious:** head tilted, one brow slightly raised, no smile yet.
- **Small smile:** eyes crease first, mouth follows. Rare. Make it count.
- **Lost:** eyes unfocused, looking past the panel. Use when a memory surfaces.
- **Determined:** jaw set, eyes steady. Only in chapter 3.

**Manga notes**
- Draw him with more negative space than Hina. He is a page waiting to be written.
- His coat should move a beat late in wind, like his shadow.
- Do not make him sparkle or glow. He is ordinary, and that is the mystery.

## Obaasan

- **Age:** 70s
- **Height:** 150 cm
- **Role:** The archive keeper, Hina's grandmother
- **Silhouette:** Small, rounded, always busy. Hands together or holding paper.
- **Hair:** Silver, in a low bun.
- **Eyes:** Dark brown, warm, knowing.
- **Outfit:** A tea-coloured cardigan, a dark skirt, house slippers.
- **Key prop:** Reading glasses she does not need, a teacup, a cloth-wrapped book.

**Expression sheet**
- **Knowing:** a small smile that does not reach her eyes.
- **Gentle:** eyes soft, head tilted.
- **Tired:** eyes lowered, hands still for once.
- **Serious:** the smile gone, face very still. Use once, in chapter 3.

## The Kura (the archive)

- A converted cedar storehouse by the sea. Three rooms, low ceiling, shelves to the rafters.
- Wooden engawa, paper shouji, tatami, a single bare bulb and lanterns.
- The north shelf holds the quiet books. The south shelf holds the drowned memories.
- Visual motifs: dust motes in lantern light, rain on the engawa, the sea through a window, stacks of paper, a ledger open on the desk.
- The archive should feel like a character: warm, old, listening.

## The books

- **Ordinary memory books:** thin, mismatched, handwritten labels.
- **Quiet books:** dull covers, no hum. Kept on the north shelf. Handle gently.
- **The drowned book:** pages made of water, rippling, cold. South shelf.
- **The blank book:** small, hand-sized, warm to the touch. No name.
- **The black book:** deep black, holds light instead of reflecting it. Hina's name inside. Part 2 hook.
