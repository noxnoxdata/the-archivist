# The Archive of Quiet Things
Part 1: The Boy Without a Book

## Premise
In a seaside town, a girl who can hear the memories inside old books meets a boy with no memory and no book of his own. Part 1 is about the week she tries to find his story before the archive loses it forever.

## Themes and tone
- Themes: memory, being remembered, choosing to stay, the quiet cost of forgetting
- Tone: Quiet, warm, slightly melancholy, hopeful. Rain, salt, old paper, small gestures.
- Ending: Ren chooses to stay for now. Hina learns she is allowed to be remembered too. A black book with her name opens the larger mystery for Part 2.

## Characters
### Hina Aizawa
- Role: Protagonist
- Voice: Dry, practical, kind underneath. Short sentences when she is scared. Notices small physical details before feelings.
- Want: To keep the archive running and to be useful enough that no one notices she is lonely.
- Fear: That she is easy to forget.
- Visual: Black hair tied back with a plain ribbon, grey eyes, a faded navy work apron over a cream sweater, ink on her fingers.

### Ren
- Role: Mystery, love interest
- Voice: Quiet, careful, unexpectedly funny. Asks precise questions. Says thank you too often.
- Want: To remember who he is.
- Fear: That he is a story someone is trying to forget.
- Visual: A faded blue coat too warm for the season, black hair falling over one eye, a shadow that lags half a second behind him.

### Obaasan
- Role: The archive keeper, Hina's grandmother
- Voice: Soft, indirect, fond of questions that are not questions.
- Want: To prepare Hina for something she will not name yet.
- Fear: That she has waited too long to tell her.
- Visual: Silver hair in a low bun, a cardigan the colour of tea, hands always busy with paper.

## World rules
- The archive is a converted storehouse by the sea.
- Every person who visits leaves a memory in the archive. The memory takes the shape of a book.
- Hina can hear the echo of a memory when she touches an old book.
- A forgotten memory goes quiet. Quiet books are kept on the north shelf and must be read gently.
- The archive does not invent memories. It only keeps what was already lived.
- The sea is not a villain. It is a keeper too, and sometimes it takes things back.

## Timeline
- Day 1: A typhoon approaches Umiura. Ren appears at the archive in the rain with no memory. (Ch 1)
- Day 2: Hina searches for Ren's book and finds a blank one. A quiet book falls. They catch a drowned memory. (Ch 2)
- Day 3: The storm peaks. They save the quiet books. Ren remembers a fragment. A black book arrives with Hina's name. (Ch 3)

## Chapter summaries
### Ch 1: The Boy in the Rain
Hina closes the archive before the typhoon. A boy in a faded blue coat arrives with no memory and no name. He says 'Ren' like a guess. His shadow lags half a second behind him. The archive goes quiet around him.

### Ch 2: The Blank Book
Hina looks for Ren's book and finds a blank one, warm to the touch. A quiet book falls from the north shelf. They catch a drowned memory of a child on a beach and a promise. Obaasan says some books are blank because someone is still writing them.

### Ch 3: What the Tide Keeps
The storm floods the archive. Hina and Ren save the quiet books. Ren remembers a voice saying 'Don't forget me.' He chooses to stay. In the morning, a black book is on the desk with Hina's name inside.
