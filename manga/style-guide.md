# Manga Style Guide: The Archive of Quiet Things

*Proper Japanese manga style. Monochrome interiors, colour cover. Right-to-left reading. Quiet, warm, melancholy.*

---

## Format

- **Page size:** B5, 182 x 257 mm, with 3 mm bleed.
- **Reading direction:** right to left, top to bottom. Panel flow must follow Japanese order.
- **Page count target:** 18 to 22 pages per chapter.
- **Panels per page:** 3 to 5. Quiet pages can use 2. Climax pages can use 5 or 6.
- **Gutter:** 6 to 8 mm between panels. Keep speech bubbles clear of the gutter.
- **Resolution for digital:** 600 dpi line art, 350 dpi tones.

## Line art

- Clean black ink. Vary line weight: heavier for foreground and silhouettes, lighter for hair and cloth.
- Hatching for shadow and texture, not airbrush gradients.
- Use screentone for midtones and skies. Avoid grey paint.
- Keep faces simple. Expression comes from eyes, brows, and mouth, not detail.

## Paneling

- Vertical emphasis. Tall panels for the archive, wide panels for the sea.
- One establishing shot per scene. One close-up per emotional beat. One quiet panel per page.
- Use negative space deliberately. Silence is a panel too.
- Do not crowd. If a page feels full, remove a panel.
- Reaction shots: eyes, hands, a held breath. Not full-body flailing.

## Screentone and texture

- Rain: fine diagonal tone plus white streaks.
- Sea: layered horizontal tone, white highlights.
- Dust motes: small white dots over a dark tone.
- Old paper: light dot tone, uneven edges.
- The drowned book: water tone that breaks the panel border. Use sparingly.

## Lettering and SFX

- Dialogue: clean rounded speech bubbles, tail toward the speaker.
- Thoughts: cloud or wavy bubble, smaller text.
- Narration: rectangular box at the panel edge, no tail.
- SFX: hand-lettered, integrated into the art. Use Japanese marks where they fit (ざあ for rain, ぱら for light rain, しん for silence), with small English equivalents if needed.
- Keep bubbles out of faces and important gestures.

## Character consistency

- Hina: small, black hair tied back, grey eyes, navy apron, cream sweater.
- Ren: tall, black hair over one eye, dark brown eyes, faded blue coat, barefoot.
- Obaasan: small, silver bun, tea-coloured cardigan.
- Keep outfits identical across Part 1. No costume changes except the rain-soaked variant.
- Use the character sheets as the single source of truth.

## Tone and references

- Reference feeling: quiet slice-of-life with a gentle supernatural thread. Think warm lamplight, rain, old books, small gestures.
- Not chibi. Not glossy digital painting. Not oversaturated colour. Not generic moe faces.
- The cover may use a muted palette: deep teal, warm amber, cream, faded navy.
- Interiors are black and white. Colour belongs on the cover and the chapter title page.

## AI-slop guardrails

If using an image model, reject outputs with:
- glossy skin, plastic highlights, oversaturated gradients;
- inconsistent hands or extra fingers;
- generic large-eyed moe faces that do not match the character sheets;
- cinematic blur or lens flare;
- text baked into the image (add lettering later).

## Page template

A reliable quiet page:
1. Wide establishing panel at the top.
2. Two medium panels for action and reaction.
3. One small close-up at the bottom for the emotional beat.

A climax page:
1. Full-bleed panel.
2. Two narrow vertical panels for speed.
3. One silent panel before the turn.
4. One large final panel with the hook.
