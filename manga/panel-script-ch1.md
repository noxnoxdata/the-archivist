# Panel Script: Chapter 1, The Boy in the Rain

*Right-to-left reading. B5 page. 8 pages. Dialogue and SFX are final drafts. Camera notes are for the artist or image model.*

---

## Page 1

**Panel 1 (wide, top, full width)**
- Camera: high angle over Umiura. Fishing boats pulled onto the breakwater. A vending machine glowing alone. Grey sea, grey sky.
- SFX: ごう (low wind).
- Narration box: "The typhoon was still three hours away when the first shutters began to rattle."

**Panel 2 (medium, right)**
- The Kura from the path. Cedar walls, paper screens, a low stone step. Rain starting.
- SFX: ぽつ ぽつ (first drops).

**Panel 3 (medium, left)**
- Hina on the engawa, tape in her teeth, pinning a paper screen. Apron, hair tied back. Eyes narrowed against the wind.
- SFX: びしっ (tape snap).

**Panel 4 (small, bottom left)**
- Close-up: her ink-stained fingers smoothing the tape.

---

## Page 2

**Panel 1 (wide, top)**
- Interior. Obaasan at the back table with tea and unnecessary glasses. Warm lamplight against the grey outside.
- Obaasan: "Leave the north window. The quiet books like the sound."
- Hina (off-panel): "The quiet books are going to get soaked."

**Panel 2 (medium, right)**
- Obaasan, eyes on her tea, a small smile.
- Obaasan: "They have waited longer than you, Hina."

**Panel 3 (medium, left)**
- Hina tapes the window anyway. Then tapes it again. A small, stubborn gesture.
- SFX: ぴっ (second strip).

**Panel 4 (small, bottom right)**
- The north shelf, out of focus. Rows of dull covers. One faint hum line above them.
- Narration box: "Every visitor leaves a memory. Every memory becomes a book."

---

## Page 3

**Panel 1 (full width, tall)**
- The rain arrives. A grey curtain across the street. The sea gone. The gutter becomes a river.
- SFX: ざあああ (heavy rain).

**Panel 2 (medium, right)**
- Hina under the eaves, arms crossed, watching the water. Small against the storm.

**Panel 3 (large, left, bottom)**
- The boy at the gate. No umbrella, no bag. Faded blue coat open over a white shirt. Black hair plastered down. Barefoot. He is looking at the Kura like a house from a dream.
- Narration box: "That was when she saw the boy."

---

## Page 4 (the key page)

**Panel 1 (wide, top)**
- Hina steps forward, mouth open to shout. Wind takes the words. Her hair pulls loose.

**Panel 2 (large, right)**
- The boy tilts his head. Rain around him. His shadow on the wet path.

**Panel 3 (large, left, the lag)**
- The same shot a heartbeat later. The boy has turned toward the gate. His shadow is still facing the old direction, a half second behind. Make the lag unmistakable but not monstrous.
- SFX: none. Silence.
- Narration box: "It was a small thing. A half second, maybe less."

**Panel 4 (small, bottom, close-up)**
- Hina's grey eyes, wide. One hand curled at her side.
- Hina (small): "Hey. You're going to drown."

---

## Page 5

**Panel 1 (medium, right)**
- The boy looks at her. Dark brown eyes, calm. Soaked and unbothered.
- Boy: "Am I?"

**Panel 2 (medium, left)**
- Hina laughs, sharp and nervous, despite herself.
- Hina: "Yes. Come in. Before the sea decides to visit."

**Panel 3 (wide, bottom)**
- He walks up the path. The rain goes through him, or seems to. His coat does not cling. The boards of the engawa do not creak under his feet.
- Narration box: "She told herself it was the rain."

---

## Page 6

**Panel 1 (wide, top)**
- Interior. The archive warm and dim. The storm a low drum behind the walls. Obaasan looks up. She does not look surprised.
- Obaasan: "Ah. You brought the rain in."

**Panel 2 (medium, right)**
- Hina and Ren at the table. Tea. Ren holds the cup with both hands and does not drink.
- Hina: "What's your name?"

**Panel 3 (large, left)**
- Ren considers. The rain fills the silence. His face is calm, but his eyes are searching.
- Ren: "Ren."

**Panel 4 (small, bottom right)**
- Hina, leaning in.
- Hina: "Is that your name, or are you guessing?"

---

## Page 7

**Panel 1 (large, right)**
- Ren looks at her. Something moves behind the calm for the first time.
- Ren: "I don't know. It came when you asked."

**Panel 2 (medium, left)**
- Obaasan sets down her cup. Her hand is steady.
- Obaasan: "And where did you come from, Ren?"

**Panel 3 (wide, bottom)**
- Ren at the window. The sea is a grey wall. His reflection is faint in the glass, and slightly behind him.
- Ren: "The rain. I think I came from the rain."

---

## Page 8 (hook)

**Panel 1 (wide, top)**
- Obaasan at the north shelf, fingers on the spines, not pulling anything out.
- Obaasan: "Then we will keep you dry, until the rain remembers what it brought."

**Panel 2 (medium, right)**
- Ren bows his head. "Thank you."

**Panel 3 (large, left, the hook)**
- The same shot, held. Ren is still bowing. His shadow on the tatami has not moved at all. Around him, the quiet books have gone silent. No hum lines. Nothing.
- Narration box: "On the north shelf, one by one, the quiet books stopped humming."

**Panel 4 (small, bottom, final)**
- Extreme close-up: Hina's hand, still holding the tape. Her fingers have gone white.
- SFX: none.
- End of chapter.
